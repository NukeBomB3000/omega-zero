Press MENU on the shelf and pick **Background**. The shelf behind the list shows each choice as you
make it.

| Row | Choices |
|---|---|
| Style | **Grid** (lines crossing, like squared paper), **Bits** (square pixels scattered like a 1-bit picture), **Solid** (the colour alone), and **Wallpaper** when the card has one |
| Motion | **Moving** (the pattern drifts slowly to the side and loops without a seam) or **Still**. Grid and Bits only |
| Colour | Night, Slate, Navy, Teal, Green, Plum, Crimson or Amber |

Left and Right change the lit row; B goes back. The default is a moving Grid in Night, close to
black.

A `.png` in `Wallpapers/` on the card is shown as long as you have not picked another style, and
stays one of the choices while the file is there.
