Press START on the shelf. Settings is a window of two panels, CONFIGS and GENERAL. Up and Down
move through both; B or START goes back to the shelf.

## CONFIGS

Left and Right change these. The lit row shows ◀ ▶ either side of its value.

| Row | Does |
|---|---|
| Fast Forward | How fast R2 runs a game: 2X, 3X, 4X or 6X |
| Fast Forward Sound | Whether you hear the game while it runs fast |
| Colour Correction | The cores' colour correction, closer to the colours of the original handheld's screen. SELECT + Y in a game does the same |
| Rumble | The motor, for games that rumble |
| Labels › | Real label scans from ScreenScraper; see [[Cart Labels]] |

## GENERAL

A opens these. Each ends in ›.

| Row | Opens |
|---|---|
| Date & Time | The clock. The row shows the date and time |
| Network | Wi-Fi, network time and SSH; see [[Wi-Fi, SSH and SFTP|Networking]]. The row shows the network joined, or ON or OFF. Handhelds without Wi-Fi don't show it |
| Background | The pattern and colour behind everything; see [[Shelf Background]] |
| About | The handheld's label, with the build's serial number |

## Date & Time

Left and Right move between the year, month, day, hour, minute and time zone; Up and Down change
the one underlined. A sets the clock. B goes back and leaves it as it was. omega-zero also asks for
the time at first boot. With **Network Time** on, the clock is kept right whenever Wi-Fi is
joined, and the time zone stays the one you set here.

## The bottom bar

On the shelf, the bar says SELECT QUICKMENU on the left and START SETTINGS on the right. In every
menu it says B RETURN and A CONFIRM. A screen's own keys are shown in the middle of it: Y FORGET
on Network, START FETCH and Y SIGN OUT on Labels.

Every move between screens slides. Going into a screen, the one you leave goes out to the right
and the new one comes in from the left; coming back is the mirror. The menus and both bars take
their colours from **Background > Colour**.
