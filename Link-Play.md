Two devices running omega-zero can link over Wi-Fi with no router: one hosts and the other joins.

1. Open the cart and pick **gpSP** for Pokémon (X on the shelf).
2. In the game, press SELECT + MENU and choose the link.
3. One player picks **HOST**, the other **JOIN**.

Pokémon Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen link, and so do both Advance Wars. mGBA's link mode plays
Mario Kart: Super Circuit, and Game Boy link plays Tetris.

A link uses the same radio as home Wi-Fi, so it takes over from it while it lasts and hands it
back when it ends.
