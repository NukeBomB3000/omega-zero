# omega-zero

omega-zero is a Game Boy-centric frontend for Anbernic H700 handhelds running
[BaseOS](https://github.com/pvaibhav/BaseOS). It plays GBA, GBC and GB games, and SNES and
PlayStation games too, from shelves of cartridges and discs that go down into the handheld when
you play them. It is made for the **RG35XX SP** first, and lays itself out natively for every
H700 panel.

## Start here

- [[Installing]]: put BaseOS and omega-zero on a card, and update later.
- [[Systems]]: the five systems, their files and cores, SNES carts and PlayStation discs.
- [[Controls]]: every button, on the shelf, in a game and in the menus. A tap of SELECT on the

## Using it

- [[Settings]]: START on the shelf. Fast-forward, rumble, the clock, the network and more.
- [[Shelf Background]]: Settings > Theme. A drifting pixel-art pattern or a plain colour behind
  the shelf, whose colour the menus take, and the bottom bar's font.
- [[Overlays]]: your own picture over the game.
- [[Link Play]]: two handhelds over Wi-Fi, for trades and battles.
- [[Wi-Fi, SSH and SFTP|Networking]]: join your network, copy games over, keep the clock right.
- [[Cart Labels]]: real label scans from ScreenScraper.
